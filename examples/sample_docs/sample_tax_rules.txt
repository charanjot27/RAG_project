SAMPLE TAX RULES (DEMO DOCUMENT — NOT REAL TAX ADVICE)

This is a small, fictional document included so you can run VeriFin end to
end before adding your own PDFs. Copy it into data/raw/ to try the pipeline:

    cp examples/sample_docs/*.txt data/raw/
    python -m src.ingest
    python -m src.index_build
    python -m src.pipeline "What is the penalty for late tax filing?"

--- Section 1: Late filing penalty ---
If a taxpayer files their annual return after the due date, a late filing
penalty of 5% of the unpaid tax is charged for each month the return is late,
up to a maximum of 25% of the unpaid tax. The penalty begins the day after the
filing due date.

--- Section 2: Standard deduction ---
The standard deduction for a single filer for the tax year is 14,600. For
married couples filing jointly, the standard deduction is 29,200. Taxpayers may
claim the standard deduction instead of itemizing their deductions.

--- Section 3: Interest on unpaid tax ---
Interest is charged on any tax that remains unpaid after the due date. The
interest rate is 8% per year, compounded daily, and applies until the balance,
including any penalties, is paid in full.

--- Section 4: Filing extensions ---
A taxpayer may request a six-month extension to file their return. An extension
to file is not an extension to pay: tax owed is still due by the original due
date, and interest accrues on any unpaid amount from that date.
